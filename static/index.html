Local-first Telegram PDF archiving

TelePDF

Archive every PDF from a public Telegram source into a folder you control. Your Telegram session stays local. Your files stay on your machine.

Local-only Telegram user session CSV index included

What the app does

  1. Save your Telegram API credentials locally.
  2. Sign in with your Telegram account.
  3. Point the app at a public source.
  4. Download every PDF into a folder on your machine.

Best for

Legal archives, research channels, publication dumps

Use it when a Telegram source has years of PDF uploads and you want one local archive instead of manual downloads.

Storage model

Direct-to-disk archiving

Downloaded files go straight to the output folder you choose. Metadata is exported as telegram_pdf_index.csv.

Safety model

No shared cloud backend

TelePDF runs as a local web app on 127.0.0.1. It is designed for self-hosted use, not as a hosted public service.

Step 1

Connect your Telegram account

Get an api_id and api_hash from my.telegram.org, then save them here.

Step 2

Start the archive job

Paste a public Telegram source and choose a destination folder on your machine.

Source rules: TelePDF expects a public source in the form @username or https://t.me/username.
